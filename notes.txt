# DD2 Recomp notes

## Setup
- ROM: Double Dragon II USA Rev 1 (same file in Mesen and recomp)
- Recomp: 100% native through Mission 1 opening route; windowed build plays with sound + PS4 pad

## Timing measurements (Mesen, Mission 1)
- Billy walks at 30 Hz: about 2 px per update, sub-pixel (occasional 3 then 1). Confirmed in 3 runs.
- Walk pose changes every 10 frames (animation, not movement).
- Punch animations update every 2nd frame. Hits freeze 3 frames (hit-stop).
- Camera scroll: 30 Hz, 2 px per update, about 1 px/frame average, same as Billy.
- Scroll has a 2,2,3,1 hitch every 8 frames, not seen in Billy sprite. Confirm in the recomp.
- HUD split at scanline 185. Do not smooth below it.
- Game sets playfield scroll during vblank, lines 247-257. Workload alternates every other frame.
- Mesen script log keeps only the last ~500 lines.
